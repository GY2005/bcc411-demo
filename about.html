Our Story · 教会历史

A growing family across two continents.

From a single congregation in Flushing to four churches on two continents, our story has been one of proclaiming the gospel and preparing workers for the harvest.

  1. 2011

    New York 纽约

    BCC411 is founded in Flushing, New York — our headquarters to this day.

  2. 2014

    Houston 休斯顿

    The ministry expands to Texas, gathering a growing congregation in Houston.

  3. 2019

    Frankfurt 法兰克福

    Our first congregation outside the U.S. begins gathering in Frankfurt, Germany.

  4. 2022

    North Carolina 北卡

    A fourth congregation is planted in Cary, North Carolina.
